Flame Editor is an advanced chart editor for Guitar Hero and Rock Band clone games.
Created with the intent of being powerful, yet intuitive. Made in C#.
Place here an example of Flame Editor

For charters, by a charter

A feature-rich chart editor with what you need (or what you used with 15 different applications)

A dedicated tool just for tempomapping, with a comprehensive user interface to place the beats where they should be.

Analyzes your charts, finds errors and helps you polish your charts with just a click.

From charts you already made, you can generate lower difficulty charts or for other similar gamemodes.

A dedicated interface just for lyrics, to place them quickly and time them perfectly.

Charts can be exported to dedicated formats for specific rhythm games, or you can export to a simple JSON.

Every single variant of a chart can be signed by a single charter to better show who made what!

What if you wanted to do an "Expert+" variant of a chart? Now you can without borrowing a different variant!

Check the optimal score of your chart and (if you want to) share the optimal strategy to the players.

If you're curious or obsessed with data, you can check other different statistics in the editor.

*inside the editor, some exporting formats don't have this feature
