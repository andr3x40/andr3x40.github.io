If your account gets breached in some way, reach out immediately to me.
You also agree you won't sell this account and that you won't create more than one account.
TL;DR: You can create an account using Google or GitHub. You login with your account and you keep this account yours and yours only. You can only have one account.

With an account, you may be able to publish, change or remove content in the website. First, you agree to not post and show hateful conduct of any kind through the services provided. This is not allowed in any way and includes, but is not limited to:
  • Discrimination by race, religion, ethnicity, sexual orientation, gender identity

Third, no political propaganda. This place is not supposed to be an echo chamber and I don't want it to become an echo chamber.
TL;DR: Be nice, don't advertise yourself at random and don't do political propaganda.
3. Termination How you can lose your account
By breaking the conditions in Paragraph 2, the owner (me) can warn, suspend or terminate your account at any time. When your account is suspended, you will lose access to your account until the suspension is lifted. If the suspension is permanent, the account will be terminated after 30 days and its data permanently deleted.
For any suspension, you have the right to appeal the decision. If the appeal is accepted, the account will be restored as soon as possible (expect at least a day of waiting, I'm slow). If it's rejected, you have only wasted your time.
TL;DR: If you mess up (see Paragraph 2 for how to mess up), you can get warned, banned temporarily or permanently. The account is deleted 30 days after a permanent ban is issued. You have the right to appeal.
